RC Circuits & Diode Clippers Practical

Conducted during the Innovation and Invention Studio class period at AU QUASAR. Exploring analog waveform shaping, time constants, and response characteristics using professional-grade lab instrumentation.

Lab Instrumentation & Equipment Used

Digital Multimeter
Fluke 15B+

Used for precise voltage, resistance, and continuity checks across components.

Storage Oscilloscope
GW Instek GDS-1102B

100MHz 2-Channel Digital Storage Oscilloscope for real-time waveform analysis and voltage measurements.

DC Power Supply
GW Instek GPE-4323A

Multi-channel linear DC power supply providing stable regulated DC voltage to test circuits.

Waveform Generator
GW Instek Generator

Used to supply input sinusoidal, square, and triangular test signals for RC response and clipper tests.

1. RC Circuit Experiments

We built and analyzed different configurations of Resistor-Capacitor (RC) circuits on breadboards using discrete resistors and capacitors. By feeding square and sinusoidal signals, we observed transient charging and discharging behaviors, measured time constants ($\tau = RC$), and verified phase shifts on the oscilloscope.

  • Component assembly & continuity testing with the Fluke 15B+
  • Waveform response analysis on the GW Instek GDS-1102B
  • DC biasing via the GW Instek GPE-4323A power supply

2. Diode Clipper Circuits

We investigated series and parallel diode clipper configurations designed to clip portions of input AC waveforms above or below specific reference voltage thresholds. Signal clipping levels were monitored live, demonstrating nonlinear circuit behavior and voltage limiting.

  • Positive and negative half-cycle clipping tests
  • Voltage threshold adjustments and diode forward-bias verification
  • Live capture of clipped waveforms on the dual-channel oscilloscope
