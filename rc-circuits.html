RC Low Pass & High Pass Filters with Diode Clippers

Conducted during the Innovation and Invention Studio class period at AU QUASAR. Exploring frequency responses of Low Pass and High Pass RC networks alongside nonlinear diode clipping characteristics using professional-grade lab instrumentation.

Lab Instrumentation & Equipment Used

Digital Multimeter
Fluke 15B+

Used for precise voltage, resistance, and continuity checks across components.

Storage Oscilloscope
GW Instek GDS-1102B

100MHz 2-Channel Digital Storage Oscilloscope for real-time waveform analysis and voltage measurements.

DC Power Supply
GW Instek GPE-4323A

Multi-channel linear DC power supply providing stable regulated DC voltage to test circuits.

Waveform Generator
OWON AG 1011F

Used to supply input sinusoidal, square, and triangular test signals across variable frequency bands.

1. RC Low Pass & High Pass Filters

We constructed and analyzed passive Low Pass (LPF) and High Pass (HPF) networks on breadboards using discrete passive components. By sweeping frequencies on the function generator, we calculated the cutoff frequency ($f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}$), verified passband gain, and observed output attenuation and phase shift.

  • Component assembly & continuity testing with the Fluke 15B+
  • Frequency response and phase shift measurement on the GW Instek GDS-1102B
  • Signal synthesis and offset control using the OWON AG1011F waveform generator

2. Diode Clipper Circuits

We investigated series and parallel diode clipper configurations designed to clip portions of input AC waveforms above or below specific reference voltage thresholds. Signal clipping levels were monitored live, demonstrating nonlinear circuit behavior and voltage limiting.

  • Positive and negative half-cycle clipping tests
  • Voltage threshold adjustments and diode forward-bias verification ($V_D \approx 0.7\text{V}$)
  • Live capture of clipped waveforms on the dual-channel oscilloscope
