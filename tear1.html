Hardware Reverse Engineering

Electric Kettle Hardware Teardown

An analytical breakdown of thermal feedback loops, bi-metallic latching mechanisms, and high-current electrical safety architectures.

Thermal & Electrical Principles

Heating principles ($P = I^2 R$). High AC current flows through the resistive heating element submerged at the vessel base, transferring thermal energy via conduction directly to the fluid mass.

The automatic cutoff mechanism relies on thermodynamic pressure differential: steam generated during boiling is channeled down a dedicated internal conduit to trigger a snap-action bi-metallic disc, physically interrupting the AC line contacts without electronic logic.

Joule Heating Formula:
$$P = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$$
Thermal Energy Input:
$$Q = m \cdot c \cdot \Delta T$$

System Architecture Components

01. Heating Element

NiCr resistive coil encapsulated in magnesium oxide inside a stainless steel tube.

02. Steam Switch

Bi-metallic disc utilizing differential thermal expansion rates to actuate snap-off switch.

03. Thermal Fuses

Secondary fail-safe thermal cutoff protection against dry boiling conditions.
